    FAQ

    Before you install.

    Do I need API keys for OpenAI or Perplexity?

    No. Capyo asks the engines through its own API access, so you need no external accounts.

    Does it add a script to my storefront?

    No. Referral tracking uses Shopify's own pixel sandbox, and the llms.txt is plain Liquid rendered by Shopify.

    What is the llms.txt file?

    A plain-text file at /llms.txt that AI crawlers read to understand your store. Capyo replaces Shopify's basic one with your products, prices, variants and availability.

    Can I remove the llms.txt?

    Yes. One click removes the template from your theme and the file reverts to Shopify's default. The file keeps working even if left behind.

    How do you measure visibility?

    Measured by asking OpenAI search and Perplexity through their APIs. AI answers vary between people and over time; this is a sample, not a ranking.

    Will this make my store appear more in AI answers?

    No app can promise that. Capyo reports what happened: which questions surfaced your store, which mentioned it, and which left you out.

    Are you affiliated with OpenAI, Perplexity or Shopify?

    No. Capyo is an independent app, not affiliated with or sponsored by OpenAI, Perplexity, Anthropic, Google or Shopify.

    What data do you keep?

    No customer names, emails, phones or addresses. Capyo keeps query text, engine answers and referral counts for the report, and deletes everything when you uninstall.
